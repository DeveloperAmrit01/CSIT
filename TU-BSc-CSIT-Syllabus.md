# TU B.Sc. CSIT syllabus (Semesters I-IV)

**Source:** `bsccsit-first-two-years454.pdf`  
**Curriculum in the PDF:** 2017; effective from academic year 2074  
**Status:** This is the first four semesters only. The document has not been verified as the current TU curriculum.

## Semester I
- CSC109 — Introduction to Information Technology (3 credits)
- CSC110 — C Programming (3 credits)
- CSC111 — Digital Logic (3 credits)
- MTH112 — Mathematics I (3 credits)
- PHY113 — Physics (3 credits)

## Semester II
- CSC160 — Discrete Structure (3 credits)
- CSC161 — Object Oriented Programming (3 credits)
- CSC162 — Microprocessor (3 credits)
- MTH163 — Mathematics II (3 credits)
- STA164 — Statistics I (3 credits)

## Semester III
- CSC206 — Data Structure and Algorithms (3 credits)
- CSC207 — Numerical Method (3 credits)
- CSC208 — Computer Architecture (3 credits)
- CSC209 — Computer Graphics (3 credits)
- STA210 — Statistics II (3 credits)

## Semester IV
- CSC257 — Theory of Computation (3 credits)
- CSC258 — Computer Networks (3 credits)
- CSC259 — Operating Systems (3 credits)
- CSC260 — Database Management System (3 credits)
- CSC261 — Artificial Intelligence (3 credits)
